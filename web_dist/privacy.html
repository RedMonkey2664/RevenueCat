HistoX — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

HistoX is a behavioural finance simulator. This policy describes exactly

what the app does with data. It is short because the app collects little.

There is no account

HistoX has no sign-up, no login and no user profile on any server. You are

not asked for your name, email address, phone number or date of birth.

What stays on your device

All of this is written to your phone's local app storage and never leaves

it unless you choose to share a card:

  • Campaign progress, scores and which levels you have cleared
  • Your run history: the calls you made, how far price had fallen at each

one, how long you took, and the score it earned

  • Your Daily Pivot votes, streak and Discipline Points
  • Your watchlist and chart preferences

Deleting the app deletes all of it. There is no backup and no restore.

What leaves your device

Market data requests. To show prices the app calls two public APIs:

  • Binance (api.binance.com) for cryptocurrency prices
  • Yahoo Finance (query1.finance.yahoo.com) for US and Indian equity

prices

These requests contain the symbol and date range being viewed. They are not

tagged with any identifier of you. Your IP address is visible to those

services, as it is to any website you visit. Their own privacy policies

apply to what they do with it.

Purchases. If you subscribe to HistoX Pro, the purchase is handled by

Apple or Google and by RevenueCat,

which manages the subscription. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated

anonymous app user ID, your purchase receipt and basic device information

such as platform and app version. HistoX never sees your payment details

and never receives your card number.

Sharing a card. If you tap Share on a result card, the image is created

on your device and handed to the share sheet you pick. HistoX does not

upload it anywhere.

What the app does not do

  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier
  • No selling or sharing of personal data, because none is collected
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo library access

Children

HistoX is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data

from anyone, including children.

Real money

HistoX never places a real trade. The Simulator uses virtual capital,

Discipline Points have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn or exchanged,

and Live Markets is read-only. The only real payment is the optional Pro

subscription, handled by the app store.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published here with a new date.

Contact

HistoX is an educational simulator. It uses virtual capital, places no real trades, and is not investment advice.
