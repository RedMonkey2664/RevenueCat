1. What HistoX is

HistoX is an educational game about behaviour during market falls. You

replay historical price series with virtual capital and choose what you

would have done. It is entertainment and self-observation.

2. What HistoX is not

HistoX is not investment advice. Nothing in it is a recommendation to

buy, sell or hold any security, cryptocurrency or other asset.

  • The app cannot place, route or execute a real trade, and never will.
  • No real money is ever at risk inside the app, and none can be won.
  • Discipline Points have no monetary value. They cannot be withdrawn,

redeemed, transferred or exchanged for anything.

  • The Daily Pivot is not betting or wagering. There is no stake and no

payout; a correct call earns in-app points only.

  • Live Markets displays real prices for reference and is read-only.

Past performance does not predict future results. A high Discipline

Score means you behaved consistently inside a replay of the past. It does

not mean you will make good decisions with real money, and it does not

measure investing skill.

The Discipline Score and the Nerve Profile describe how you played this

game. They are not a psychological, clinical or financial assessment.

3. Historical data and the "optimal move"

Price series come from public market data providers. Data may be delayed,

incomplete or revised, and we do not warrant its accuracy.

The "historically optimal move" shown in the Debrief is a **hindsight

benchmark**: it is computed after the fact, with knowledge of what price did

next. It is not a signal, a strategy or something that could have been known

at the time. Where the data does not support a clear answer, the app says so

and does not score the moment.
