# Demo video — 90-second shot list

For the Shipaton submission. Nothing here is faked: every shot is the real
app, and the numbers are whatever the run actually produces.

**Target:** 90 seconds, under the 2-minute limit. Portrait phone capture,
1179×2556. No music under the voiceover, or it will fight the words.

**Before recording:** clear app data so progress starts empty, then play two
levels beforehand so the Debrief's YOUR PATTERN panel has history to compare
against. Use a build with RevenueCat keys so the paywall shows real prices.

---

## 0:00–0:10 · The hook

**Shot:** Campaign map, then tap the amber PLAY node. Level briefing appears:
"YOU ARE ALREADY INVESTED. THE FALL HAS BEGUN." Tap START RUN.

**Voiceover:** "Everyone thinks they'd hold through a crash. HistoX drops you
into a real one and finds out."

**On screen:** nothing added. The briefing says it.

## 0:10–0:28 · The decision

**Shot:** The tape rolls. Let it run to the halt — the screen goes warm red,
"HALTED · CALL 1 OF 14", the drawdown chip reads about −8%. **Hold two full
seconds without tapping.** That pause is the product.

**Voiceover:** "You're down eight percent. You don't know the asset, you
don't know the date, and there's no timer. Hold, sell, or buy the dip."

**On screen:** "The asset and the dates are hidden. You play it blind."

## 0:28–0:40 · The consequence

**Shot:** Tap SELL ALL. The tape continues and price keeps falling, then
recovers past where you sold. Let that play.

**Voiceover:** "Sell, and the loss stops being on paper."

## 0:40–0:58 · The Debrief

**Shot:** Debrief. Discipline Score, then expand CALL BREAKDOWN to show one
row with its real outcome line, then YOUR PATTERN.

**Voiceover:** "Afterwards it shows what each call cost you — and how this
run compares with your last ones. Sold into three of four falls before; one
this time."

**On screen:** "Every number comes from your own runs."

## 0:58–1:10 · The other pillars

**Shot:** Three quick cuts, about four seconds each.
1. Daily Pivot: the BTC question, tap YES, the sealed state.
2. Time Machine: enter an amount, the result card renders.
3. Live Markets: the watchlist with source tags visible.

**Voiceover:** "A daily call on Bitcoin. A what-if calculator you can share.
Real prices, read-only — the app can't place a trade."

## 1:10–1:25 · Pro and RevenueCat

**Shot:** Tap a locked PRO node. Paywall with real store prices, yearly and
monthly. Show Restore, Terms and Privacy at the bottom. Complete a sandbox
purchase; the level unlocks.

**Voiceover:** "Pro opens fifteen more crashes. Subscriptions run on
RevenueCat — one entitlement, restore across devices, and the paywall reads
its prices straight from the store."

## 1:25–1:30 · Close

**Shot:** Campaign map with the newly cleared node and its stars.

**Voiceover:** "HistoX. Find out how you behave before it costs you
anything."

**On screen:** "Educational simulator · virtual capital · not investment
advice"

## Rules while recording

- Never say or imply the app predicts markets, or that a score means
  investing skill.
- Keep the SIMULATED badge in frame whenever money-shaped numbers show.
- Don't speed up the halt. The hesitation is the point.
- If a take produces an embarrassing score, keep it. A panicked run is a
  better demo than a perfect one.
